## Scope
### In Scope
- Typed authorization context, deny-by-default route classification, explicit scopes, roles, tenant, auth method, and agent identity.
- OAuth registrar owner/tenant binding, scope attenuation, token-use separation, and one-time atomic legacy authority upgrade.
- Live-identity JWT verification with signed tenant and identity generation so suspended/revoked tokens fail immediately and cannot resurrect after reactivation.
- Cross-tenant replay denial for JWT, delegation, scoped bearer, and agent credentials.
- Persisted owner/tenant authorization for identities, SCIM, delegation chains, revocations, and secret-vault resources.
- Explicit administrator/owner quarantine listing and assignment, append-only durable evidence, readiness blocking, and restart persistence.
- Behavioral rate-limit, timeout, readiness, startup-contract, package-import, and one-worker/one-replica deployment-containment checks.
- Deterministic CI/test isolation, strict dependency auditing, and exact release evidence.

### Out of Scope
- Postgres/Redis migration and multi-replica or multi-worker support outside the atomic OAuth authority transition.
- Real MCP/A2A invocation, managed workload execution, and production provider proof.
- Billing, marketplace, or autonomous procurement activation.
- Production deployment, hosted rollback, or public-environment verification.
- Marketing and dashboard redesign.
